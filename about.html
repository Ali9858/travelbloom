Company History

TravelBloom was founded in 2022 with the idea of making travel discovery easier and more inspiring for everyone.

What started as a small recommendation platform quickly grew into a travel experience website helping users explore beaches, temples, cities, and unique destinations around the world.

Today, TravelBloom continues to provide beautiful travel ideas, personalized recommendations, and simple tools for planning unforgettable journeys.
